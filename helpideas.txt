TULOY: IDEAS
============

Things we could add, grouped by what they'd fix. Each has a rough size
(S = an hour or two, M = a day, L = several days) and a note on why it
might be worth it. Nothing here is built yet. The list at the bottom says
what was just added, so we don't suggest it twice.

Pick by the stars: *** = would help most people right away.


1. GETTING STARTED AND HELP
---------------------------

*** Sign in without leaving Tuloy (M)
    Today "Sign In…" opens Terminal. Tuloy could run Claude Code's sign-in
    itself and just open the browser page, so there's no Terminal at all.
    Needs testing: the sign-in may want a real terminal.

*** A "what just happened" line after every action (S)
    After Ping Now, Pause, or changing the schedule, show one sentence in
    the window: "Pinged. Fresh window at 4:43 PM." No guessing whether a
    click did anything.

**  Help buttons next to things (S)
    A small (?) beside each setting that opens the matching Help answer,
    instead of making people go and look for it.

**  Search in Help (S)
    A search box over the questions. Worth it once there are 15 or more.

**  A short tour on first open (M)
    Three coach marks pointing at the menu bar icon, Ping Now and the mini
    window. For people who skip the welcome.

*   A "Copy details for support" button (S)
    Copies Claude Code's version, the last five pings and the current
    settings (never the login) so someone asking for help can paste them.

*   Help in Filipino (M)
    The name is Filipino. A Tagalog translation of the app would suit it.


2. CONNECTION
-------------

*** Usage for desktop-app and claude.ai users (M-L)
    Live usage only updates from Claude Code in Terminal. People who use
    the Claude desktop app or the website see a countdown but rarely a
    percentage. Look for another honest source: for example, asking Claude
    Code for its usage report every so often, which costs no tokens if the
    command works without a conversation. Needs a real signed-in test.

**  Tell people when Claude Code updated (S)
    Remember Claude Code's version at each good ping. If pings start
    failing right after an update, say so: "This began after Claude Code
    updated to 2.2.0."

**  A health check on a timer (S)
    Run the free sign-in check each morning an hour before the ping, and
    notify if it fails, so there's time to fix it before the ping matters.

**  One-click install of Claude Code (M)
    A button that runs the official installer and shows its progress, for
    people who have never opened Terminal.

*   Choose which Claude Code to use (S)
    For people with more than one installed: pick the path in Connection.


3. WINDOWS AND THE MENU BAR
---------------------------

*** A real popover from the menu bar icon (M)
    Replace the plain menu with a small panel like the mini window: the
    ring, the countdown, and big Ping Now / Pause buttons, right under the
    icon.

**  A Dock icon while a window is open (S-M)
    Tuloy has no Dock icon, so its windows can get lost behind others.
    Show one while the main window is open and hide it again after.

**  Mini window options (S)
    Sizes (tiny ring only, or the current one), see-through when the mouse
    isn't over it, and "stay on this desktop only".

**  A desktop widget (L)
    The ring and countdown as a macOS widget, on the desktop or in
    Notification Centre. Needs a widget extension, so a real Xcode project.

*   A keyboard shortcut for Ping Now (S)
    A system-wide shortcut, chosen in Preferences.

*   Themes and an accent colour (M)
    Goes with the logo work: a Tuloy colour, light and dark, and maybe two
    or three ring styles.


4. SCHEDULE
-----------

*** Suggest a fresh time (M)
    "You usually start around 9:10. Try a fresh window at 11:00?" Worked
    out from when your windows actually open.

**  Different times on different days (M)
    An early start on weekdays, a late one on Saturday.

**  Skip a day (S)
    "Skip tomorrow" or "Skip until Monday", for holidays, without changing
    the schedule.

**  Whole hours only, if Claude rounds to the hour (S)
    If real pings show that windows start on the hour, make the time
    picker offer hours only, so what you pick is what you get.

*   Calendar awareness (L)
    Don't burn a window on a day your calendar says you're out.

*   Pause until (S)
    "Pause for 2 hours" or "until tomorrow", instead of a pause you have
    to remember to undo.


5. KNOWING YOUR USAGE
---------------------

*** A history chart (M)
    A week of windows as bars: when each opened, how full it got. Shows
    whether the schedule fits how you really work.

**  A "you're nearly out" notification (S)
    At 80% and 95% of a window, with the time left until the reset.

**  A weekly limit forecast (M)
    "At this pace you'll use your week by Thursday afternoon."

**  Export pings and usage (S)
    A CSV file of everything Tuloy has recorded, for people who like
    spreadsheets.

*   Token count per day and week (S)
    Add up what the pings themselves cost, to show it really is tiny.


6. NOTIFICATIONS
----------------

**  A heads-up before the reset (S)
    "Fresh window in 10 minutes", for people who want to save a big task
    for the fresh allowance.

**  Quiet hours (S)
    No notifications at night, even if a window resets then.

*   Notification actions (S)
    "Ping Now" and "Pause" buttons right on the notification.


7. WHEN THE MAC IS ASLEEP
-------------------------

*** A ready-made iPhone Shortcut (S)
    A link in Help that adds the automation in one tap, instead of five
    steps. (We decided the phone, not waking the Mac, is the answer here.)

**  A QR code in Help (S)
    Point the phone at the screen to get that Shortcut.

*   "Your Mac was asleep at ping time" reminder (S)
    If the scheduled ping is missed three days running, suggest the iPhone
    Shortcut.


8. TRUST AND SAFETY
-------------------

**  A "what Tuloy has done" log (S)
    A plain list in Help: every ping, every file it changed and when.
    Mostly there already as Recent pings; this adds the file changes.

**  Show the exact command before the first ping (S)
    In the welcome: "This is everything Tuloy will ever send", with the
    command, before anyone presses Send Test Ping.

*   Uninstall button (S)
    Disconnect live usage, remove ~/.tuloy, turn off launch at login, and
    say what's left to drag to the Bin.


9. SHARING TULOY WITH OTHER PEOPLE
----------------------------------

*** Sign and notarise the app (M)
    With the Apple Developer account, so other Macs open it without a
    warning. Needed before giving it to anyone.

**  A download page and automatic updates (M-L)
    A small site with the app, and "Check for Updates" in Preferences.

**  A Homebrew install (S, after signing)
    brew install --cask tuloy.

*   An app icon and menu bar glyph (M)
    Planned already, with the theme.


10. NICE TO HAVE
----------------

*   A streak (S)
    "12 days of fresh windows in a row." Light encouragement.

*   A message other than "hi" (S)
    Let people choose the ping text. Purely for fun: it costs the same.

*   More than one account (L)
    A work plan and a personal plan, each with its own schedule.
